A waifu collector that respects your time — and a 7x7 battlefield that respects your brain.

Neon Valkyries is an anime idle RPG set in a neon-drenched cyberpunk world. Summon over 100 hand-drawn heroines, each with Live2D animation and full voice acting, then place them on a 7x7 grid where position decides who lives.

WHY PLAYERS STAY

✨ Every heroine is animated, not a still image
Pulling a card unlocks a breathing, blinking, fully voiced character — plus an intimacy route with hidden scenes you can only see by raising her bond. The whole roster is animated, not just the poster girl.

⚔️ Position beats raw power
Your 7x7 grid is the whole game. Front, back, left, right: each square changes a heroine's role, skill coverage and survivability. Two players with identical rosters get different results.

🔁 Idle when you want, tactics when you don't
Offline farming keeps your team growing while you are away — real AFK progression, not a timer you have to babysit. No energy walls, no forced daily chores: log in, spend ten minutes on a real decision, log out.

🧩 Team building that actually matters
Factions and classes combine into bonds and buffs you have to plan around. Push the PvE campaign for story, climb the tower for a pure formation test, then take the same team into the PvP arena and find out whether your read on the meta was right.

📖 A story worth reading
A full campaign plus a personal chronicle and intimacy trial for every deployed heroine. The main plot is not a tutorial wrapper.

💎 What "free to play" actually means here
• High SSR rates with a transparent pity counter, shown in the banner before you pull
• Free base roster from events, so a collector can finish the index without paying
• The entire main story and all PvP arena rewards are open to every account. No paywall, no ad gate.

🧭 HOW TO START
You do not need to reroll for hours. The free roster from events is strong enough to clear the early campaign, and the pity counter means a limited banner cannot quietly slip away. If you have played a gacha game before, you will know what to do within ten minutes.

🎯 Who this is for
• Gacha and waifu collector players who want a roster worth keeping
• Turn-based tactics fans who are tired of auto-battlers that play themselves
